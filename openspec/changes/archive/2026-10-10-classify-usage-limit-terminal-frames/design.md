# Design

One shared classifier recognizes explicit usage_limit_reached codes and usage-limit messages on normalized upstream_error envelopes. Explicit request-shaped or otherwise classified codes retain their meaning. HTTP, WebSocket, health and coalesced refresh paths use that predicate.

Pre-visible exhaustion may walk eligible accounts. After visible output, health is updated but replay remains forbidden. API-key settlement precedes deferred health writes. The existing refresh task ownership, debounce and eligibility rules remain in effect. Tests exercise public routes and the actual stream frame handling, including unrelated-message and explicit-code controls.
